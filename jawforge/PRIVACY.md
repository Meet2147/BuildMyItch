# JawForge Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 2026_

JawForge is built to know as little about you as possible.

## What happens to your photos

Face scans are analyzed **entirely on your device** using Apple's Vision
framework. The photo is processed in memory and discarded immediately after
analysis. **No photo ever leaves your phone**, and none is stored by the app.

## What the app stores (on your device only)

- The numeric results of your scans (jaw angle, width, proportion, symmetry
  and the derived score) with their dates
- Your onboarding answers (age range, lifestyle and training preferences,
  optional height/weight, goal)
- Exercise completion history and streaks

All of this lives in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the
app deletes it. It is never uploaded — JawForge has **no servers, no
accounts, and no analytics SDKs**.

## What we collect

Nothing. JawForge does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
data.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
JawForge only receives Apple's anonymous confirmation that your Apple
Account owns a Pro entitlement. Billing and refunds are handled entirely by
Apple under their terms.

## Health disclaimer

JawForge is a fitness aid, not a medical device, and its measurements are
photo-based estimates. It provides no medical advice.
